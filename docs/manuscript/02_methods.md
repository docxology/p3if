# Methods

The native CLI migrates the shipped ScienceResearch domain into the
interchange format. The replay analyzes its validated snapshot to count
declared identities, linked identities, and distinct coordinates. The
denominator for each space is the product of its participating axis
sizes. Citation links and corpus values are retained with the source.
[ScienceResearch dataset](https://github.com/docxology/p3if/blob/d567b943378ebbc6a24ff92ab90fed79a09d274e/data/domains/scienceresearch.json)

A separate revision begins with the synthetic intake fixture. The script
adds a process, points the existing triadic relationship at that process,
and removes its reported strength of zero. The comparison uses the same
framework identity on both sides. The before and after reports record
their semantic hashes and the changed fields. [Synthetic fixture](https://github.com/docxology/p3if/blob/d567b943378ebbc6a24ff92ab90fed79a09d274e/examples/interchange/framework-v1.json)
