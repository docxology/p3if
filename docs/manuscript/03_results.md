# Results

The retained ScienceResearch dataset contains 71 relationships covering
71 distinct triple coordinates out of 112,500 possible positions. Its
linked and unlinked identities are shown in the figure.

![Stacked bars show linked and unlinked properties, processes, and perspectives in the ScienceResearch dataset.](../../output/figures/declared-coverage.png){#fig:declared-coverage}

The synthetic revision adds one process and rewires one existing
relationship. Its observed triple count remains one. The possible triple
count grows from two to four as the process axis gains an identity. The
strength report changes from one reported zero and one omitted value to
two omitted values. The comparison identifies the changed endpoint and
strength field on the same relationship.
